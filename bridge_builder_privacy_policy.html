1. Introduction

Welcome to Bridge Builder: Master Architect, developed and published by Codexat Games ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, safe, and enjoyable gaming experience for players of all ages worldwide.

This Privacy Policy explains how our game collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you install and play Bridge Builder on mobile devices.

🔍

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store personal identifying information such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, or biometric data.

Local Device Storage (Offline Play): Game progress, unlocked levels (1–30), earned stars, custom bridge design blueprints, and audio/haptic settings are stored 100% locally on your device via standard private app preferences.

The game and integrated third-party SDKs may automatically access limited non-personal technical data, including:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Network Status: Network connectivity state (WiFi/Mobile data) to check remote configuration and deliver advertisements.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA for ad attribution and frequency capping.
📢

3. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To provide free access to all game levels, Bridge Builder uses reputable third-party advertising partners:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to display Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Survey Ads. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and usage data according to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.
  • GitHub Remote Configuration: We securely host remote game parameters (maintenance flags, version update announcements, and ad frequency) via static JSON on GitHub Pages. No personal user data is sent to or stored on GitHub servers.
⚙️

4. App Permissions Explained

Bridge Builder requests only minimal permissions necessary for standard gameplay:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for fetching game update notices, maintenance status, and loading AdMob ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify internet availability before making network calls, ensuring seamless offline mode.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively for immersive haptic tactile feedback when placing bridge joints, snapping beams, and vehicle crossings (can be toggled in Game Settings).
🛡️

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is family-friendly and suitable for general audiences. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

🌐

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have specific rights regarding your data:

  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt-out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset/Delete Advertising ID).
  • Data Deletion: You can erase all local game data at any time by clearing the game cache/storage in your device's App Manager or uninstalling the app.
