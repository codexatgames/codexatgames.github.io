MediaVault Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

Privacy First

1. Introduction

Welcome to MediaVault ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates, what permissions it requests, and how media content is handled on your device.

2. Local Storage & Zero Data Collection

MediaVault operates on a strict Local Storage Privacy Model:

  • No Media Uploads: All WhatsApp statuses, downloaded images, and videos are saved and stored strictly on your local device storage (`/Download/MediaVault`).
  • No External Server Transfers: We do NOT collect, upload, transmit, or store your private media files, contact lists, or messages on any external server.
  • No User Account Required: You can use MediaVault without signing up or providing personal account details.

3. Permissions & Purpose

To provide status saving and media downloader capabilities, MediaVault requests specific Android system permissions:

  • Storage Access / All Files Access (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): Required to scan local WhatsApp & WhatsApp Business status directories (`.Statuses`) and save media files to your device gallery upon your request.
  • Internet Access (`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Required to fetch remote app configurations, verify UMP user consent, and load non-intrusive advertisements via Google Mobile Ads SDK.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

MediaVault uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Third-party vendor services used in the app include:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Google Ads)

Google Mobile Ads may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as Device IDs or advertising identifiers) to serve ads, manage ad frequency, and prevent fraud in compliance with Google AdMob Policies and GDPR UMP Consent Framework.

5. GDPR & User Consent Choices

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, MediaVault presents a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form upon app launch. You can modify your ad consent choices at any time through the in-app Settings screen.

6. Children's Privacy

MediaVault does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

7. Disclaimer

MediaVault is an independent utility application developed to assist users in saving their own media. MediaVault is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms Inc. Users are solely responsible for obtaining permission from media owners before saving or sharing copyright-protected content.
