1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Hill Craze 2D ("we", "our", or "the Game"). We are dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of all players.

This Privacy Policy explains how our game operates, what data is processed when you download, install, and enjoy Hill Craze 2D, and how we adhere to worldwide privacy standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Google Play Developer Policies.

4. Advertising & GDPR / UMP Consent

Advertisements keep Hill Craze 2D completely free to play for millions of racers worldwide. We display ads through Google AdMob in full compliance with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF).

Consent Management (EEA & UK Players):

  • When starting the game in regions subject to GDPR, you will receive a Google-certified consent dialog before any ad is rendered.
  • You retain the absolute freedom to accept personalized ads, consent to non-personalized ads, or configure vendor partners individually.
  • You can revoke or update your consent preferences anytime via your device Settings > Google > Ads, or by resetting your Advertising ID.

Rewarded Ads:

Watching rewarded videos (e.g. for bonus coins, lucky spin tickets, or reviving out-of-fuel vehicles) is 100% voluntary. No video is played without your explicit tap on the reward button.

5. Data Security, Anti-Tamper & Integrity

We treat your game progress and fairness with enterprise-grade protection:

  • Cryptographic Local Encryption: All game save files (containing your coins, gems, trophies, and upgrades) are encrypted using algorithmic ciphers with dynamic salts and Base64Url encoding before writing to device storage.
  • HMAC-SHA256 Integrity Signatures: Every saved state is cryptographically signed. Tampering with internal storage files or using memory injection tools automatically invalidates the signature and prevents corrupted saves.
  • ProGuard / R8 Code Hardening: The application release binary is hardened using ProGuard and R8 aggressive name-mangling and class flattening to protect against decompilation, reverse-engineering, and unauthorized modifications.
  • Local Fair-Play Integrity: The game executes lightweight local checks in device memory to protect economy balance and prevent runtime tampering. No private diagnostic logs or telemetry are uploaded to external servers.
🎮

6. Age Policy & General Audience Protection

Hill Craze 2D is developed as casual off-road arcade entertainment for a general audience. We do not design our game specifically for children under 13, nor do we knowingly collect or maintain personal identifying information from underage players.

  • No Account Registration: The game functions entirely without user accounts, profiles, emails, phone numbers, or social media logins.
  • Local Data Storage: All race progress, unlocked vehicles, and high scores are saved locally on your device rather than personal cloud databases.
  • Ad Network Standards: Advertisements delivered through Google AdMob adhere strictly to industry privacy standards and general audience safety policies.
  • Parental Contact: If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal data through support communication, please contact us at support@codexgame.com to request immediate removal.
