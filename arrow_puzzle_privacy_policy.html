2. What this App does not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. The App does not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location
  • payment card details (there is no in-app purchase checkout in the App)

Game progress stays on your device. We do not operate a player database of personal profiles.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the App saves the following locally (using on-device preferences). This data stays on your phone or tablet unless you uninstall the App or clear its storage:

  • level progress, stars, coins, unlocked skins, and power-up inventory
  • sound and haptic settings
  • a cached copy of the App’s remote ads configuration

We do not upload this gameplay data to our own servers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads. The App may show banner, interstitial (full-screen), rewarded, native, and app-open ads.

To deliver, measure, and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google and its partners may collect or receive:

  • advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID / Apple Identifier for Advertisers)
  • device and app information (device type, OS version, language, app version, time zone)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP
  • ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video views)
  • diagnostics such as crash and ad-request errors

This advertising data is processed by Google under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Consent, tracking, and your choices

In regions where required (including the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the App shows Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before personalized ads are requested. You can change advertising consent later from the App’s Settings screen when privacy options are required.

You can also limit ads at the device level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete advertising ID
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off tracking for this App (or Allow Apps to Request to Track)

If you decline tracking or personalized ads, you can still play. You may still see ads, but they may be less relevant (non-personalized ads).

Rewarded ads are optional. You choose to watch them in exchange for in-game coins. The game remains playable if you skip rewarded ads.

6. Other network requests

Besides ads, the App may connect to the internet for:

  • Remote ads configuration from codexatgames.github.io (ad on/off flags and ad unit settings — not your personal profile)
  • Fonts loaded by the Google Fonts service so the interface can render correctly

7. Permissions

The App requests only what it needs to run and show ads:

  • Internet / network state — to load the game’s remote config, fonts, and ads
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by Google advertising services
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS) — asked so ads can be measured or personalized if you allow it

The App does not request contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location permission.

8. Children

Arrow Puzzle: Tap Away is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under the digital-consent age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through ads or the device, contact us and we will help as far as we reasonably can.

9. Data retention

10. International processing

For requests about data held by Google, use Google’s tools and privacy pages linked above, or email us and we will point you to the right place.
