Privacy & Data Governance Policy
Package Name: com.wittlegames.wittledefender • Effective Date: September 18, 2026

🛡️ 1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Wittle Defender: Castle Siege (the "App"), developed and published by Wittle Games ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to providing an entertaining gaming experience while treating player privacy with the highest standards of transparency and integrity.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play our game. Wittle Defender is designed as an offline-first single-player roguelike game, and we do not collect, store, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.

💾 2. Local Game Progression & Data Storage

All game progression data—including unlocked heroes, talent tree upgrades, astral crystals, gold vault balances, high scores, wave records, and settings—is stored exclusively locally on your device storage via secured local preferences.

✦ We do not require player account creation, social login, or cloud authentication.
✦ You retain complete ownership of your save data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game will remove local game files.

📢 3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

To keep Wittle Defender free to play, the game integrates trusted industry-standard third-party SDKs for advertising and basic app health analytics:

  • Google AdMob & Google Mobile Ads SDK: Used to display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, rewarded video, and app open advertisements. AdMob may process anonymous device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA) to serve compliant, non-personalized or personalized ads depending on regional consent.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP SDK): Provides explicit consent management forms for players located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland in compliance with GDPR.
  • Google Play Services: Used for platform compatibility, security verification, and game updates.

🌐 4. Global Regulatory Compliance

🇪🇺 GDPR (EU & UK) Compliant with Google UMP consent framework. Players can review or revoke consent choices at any time via the in-game Privacy Options.
🇺🇸 CCPA / CPRA (US) We do not sell or share personal information. We honor Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.
🛡️ COPPA & Google Families Ad content is strictly capped at PG rating (`maxAdContentRating: pg`). No sensitive or age-inappropriate ads are served.

👶 5. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's online privacy is of paramount importance to us. Wittle Defender does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

If a parent or guardian believes that any information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly rectify the situation.

🔒 6. Security & Game Integrity

We implement robust administrative, technical, and platform security measures to protect the integrity of the application:

  • R8 / ProGuard code obfuscation and native binary stripping to prevent unauthorized tampering.
  • Anti-tamper security policies that enforce fair play across all game modes.
  • Zero background data tracking or unauthorized telemetry collection.

⚙️ 7. Your Privacy Rights & Opt-Out Choices

You have full control over your privacy settings:

  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising Identifier at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID.
  • Opt-out of Personalized Ads: Toggle off personalized advertising in your Android / iOS system settings.
  • In-Game Consent: Access the in-game settings / privacy button to adjust or review your consent choices.
