Privacy Policy

Spellforge by Codexat Games
Effective date: 15 September 2026
Last updated: 15 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Codexat Games (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Spellforge mobile app (package com.spellforge.spellforge).

Spellforge is a free math puzzle game. You do not create an account. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we store on your device

Game progress is saved locally on your device with on-device preferences, including:

  • language choice (English / Gujarati)
  • campaign and Unforge stars, unlocks, and clear status
  • Wild Magic streaks
  • a cached copy of remote ads settings

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the app, clear app data, or your device backups include app data.

2. Information collected by advertising partners

Spellforge shows ads through Google AdMob so the game can stay free. Google and its partners may collect or receive:

  • advertising ID / device identifiers
  • IP address, device type, OS version, and approximate location derived from IP
  • ad views, clicks, and crash/diagnostics related to ads
  • cookie or similar technology data on some devices

Google may use this information to serve ads, measure performance, and (where allowed) show more relevant ads. See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking / Apple Advertising).

3. European users (GDPR / UMP)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another region where consent is required, Spellforge may show Google’s official consent message before personalized ads. That form is shown only where Google determines consent is required. It is not shown in countries such as India.

You can change advertising choices later through your device settings or, where Google requires it, through the privacy options in the consent form.

4. Network requests we make

The app may connect to the internet to:

  • load ads from Google
  • fetch ads configuration from codexatgames.github.io (ad unit IDs and on/off flags only — not your name or email)
  • download fonts used in the interface (Google Fonts)

5. Children

Spellforge is an educational puzzle for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe a child provided personal data through ads or the device, contact us and we will help you request deletion from the relevant partner where possible.

6. Data sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the app:

  • Google (AdMob / advertising and measurement)
  • your device OS vendor (Android / iOS system services)
  • if required by law

We do not sell player lists or game progress.

7. Data retention

Local game data remains until you delete the app or clear storage. Advertising data is retained by Google according to Google’s policies.

8. Security

We use standard platform storage and HTTPS for configuration and ads. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

  • Uninstall the app or clear app data to remove local progress
  • Reset or opt out of advertising ID in system settings
  • EEA users: use Google’s consent / privacy options when shown
